# BirrGo Auto Poster --- Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 24, 2026\
**Website:** https://shiqela.online

This Privacy Policy explains how BirrGo Auto Poster may collect, use,
store, and share information when you use our website and services.

## 1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect: - Account details,
such as your email address. - Information about social media accounts
you choose to connect. - Authorization tokens and permissions needed to
provide connected-platform features. - Videos, images, captions, and
other content you choose to upload or publish. - Technical information,
such as device, browser, log, and diagnostic information.

Only describe information that your live service actually collects.
Update this policy if your implementation differs.

## 2. How we use information

We may use information to: - Provide and operate the publishing
service. - Connect social media accounts at your request. - Process
content publishing requests. - Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the
service. - Protect the service and users against fraud, abuse, and
unauthorized access. - Respond to support requests and meet legal
obligations.

## 3. Social media integrations

If you connect a TikTok, Facebook, or other supported account, BirrGo
uses the permissions you authorize to provide the relevant integration
features. The information exchanged with a platform is subject to that
platform's terms and privacy policy as well as the permissions you
grant.

You can review the privacy policies of the platforms you connect. BirrGo
does not control how third-party platforms independently handle
information.

## 4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. We may share or transmit
information: - With a social media platform when needed to carry out an
action you request. - With service providers that help host, secure, or
operate BirrGo. - When required by law or when necessary to protect
rights, safety, and the service.

Your actual service providers and data flows should be reflected in this
section.

## 5. Data security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information. No
internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be
completely secure. Keep access credentials and API secrets confidential.

## 6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, meet
legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. Retention
periods may depend on the type of information and how you use BirrGo.

## 7. Disconnecting accounts and deleting information

You may disconnect a social media account through the available account
settings, where provided. You may request deletion of personal
information by contacting BirrGo through the contact details published
on https://shiqela.online. We may need to retain certain information
where required or permitted by law.

Before launch, publish a working process for receiving and handling
deletion requests, and make sure it matches your actual implementation.

## 8. Children's privacy

BirrGo is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a
child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the
request.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version
will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests relating to personal information,
contact the BirrGo team through the contact details published on
https://shiqela.online.
